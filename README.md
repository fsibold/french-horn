# Air French Horn

Play a French horn with your camera: open or pucker your mouth to blow, move your hand up and down to change notes, and curl your index, middle, and ring fingers to press the three valves.

Play it at https://fsibold.github.io/french-horn/ in Chrome and allow the camera. Everything is in `index.html`; the settings to change are at the top of its script.

Built with MediaPipe hand and face tracking and Tone.js.
